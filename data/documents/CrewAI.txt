CrewAI

Definition

CrewAI is a framework for building AI systems where multiple AI agents work together to complete tasks.

How it Works

Each AI agent has a specific role, such as researcher, writer, or reviewer. Agents collaborate to solve complex problems.

Key Points

• Multi-agent architecture.
• Role-based task assignment.
• Supports collaboration.
• Improves task efficiency.

Applications

• Research Automation
• Business Workflows
• Content Generation
• Software Development

Example

One agent gathers information, another writes the report, and a third reviews the final output.

Summary

CrewAI enables multiple AI agents to collaborate and solve complex tasks efficiently.
