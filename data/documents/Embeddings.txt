Embeddings

Definition

Embeddings are numerical vector representations of text, images, or other data. They capture the semantic meaning of the data.

How it Works

An embedding model converts text into a vector. Similar texts produce vectors that are close together in vector space.

Key Points

• Represent semantic meaning.
• Used for similarity search.
• Essential for RAG systems.
• Enable efficient vector retrieval.

Applications

• Semantic Search
• Recommendation Systems
• Document Retrieval
• Question Answering

Example

The sentences "AI is amazing" and "Artificial Intelligence is powerful" produce similar embeddings.

Summary

Embeddings convert text into vectors, allowing computers to understand semantic similarity.
