BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers)

Definition

BERT is a Transformer-based language model developed by Google. It understands the meaning of words by considering both the left and right context of a sentence.

How it Works

BERT is pre-trained on a large text corpus using Masked Language Modeling (MLM) and Next Sentence Prediction (NSP). It can then be fine-tuned for tasks like text classification and question answering.

Key Points

• Developed by Google.
• Uses only the Transformer Encoder.
• Understands bidirectional context.
• Produces contextual word embeddings.

Applications

• Question Answering
• Sentiment Analysis
• Named Entity Recognition
• Search Engines

Example

In the sentence "He went to the bank to deposit money," BERT understands that "bank" refers to a financial institution.

Summary

BERT is a powerful language model that understands the context of words, improving the accuracy of many NLP tasks.
