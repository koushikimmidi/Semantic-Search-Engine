Sentence Transformers

Definition

Sentence Transformers are models that generate embeddings for entire sentences or paragraphs instead of individual words.

How it Works

The model processes the input sentence and produces a fixed-length vector representing its meaning.

Key Points

• Generates sentence-level embeddings.
• Faster than comparing raw text.
• Supports semantic similarity.
• Commonly used with FAISS.

Applications

• Semantic Search
• Duplicate Detection
• Question Answering
• Document Clustering

Example

Sentence Transformers help find documents with similar meanings even if different words are used.

Summary

Sentence Transformers create meaningful sentence embeddings that improve semantic search and retrieval.
