Vector Databases

Definition

A Vector Database stores and indexes embeddings to enable fast similarity search over large datasets.

How it Works

Documents are converted into embeddings and stored in the database. User queries are also converted into embeddings, and the database returns the most similar vectors.

Popular Vector Databases

• Pinecone
• Milvus
• Weaviate
• Chroma

Applications

• RAG Systems
• Semantic Search
• Recommendation Systems
• AI Assistants

Example

An enterprise chatbot retrieves company documents from a vector database before generating an answer.

Summary

Vector Databases are specialized systems designed for storing and searching embeddings efficiently, making them a core component of modern AI applications.
