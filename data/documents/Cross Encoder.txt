Cross Encoder

Definition

A Cross Encoder is a deep learning model used to rerank search results by evaluating the relationship between a query and a document together.

How it Works

Unlike embedding models that encode the query and document separately, a Cross Encoder processes both simultaneously. It analyzes their interaction and assigns a relevance score.

Key Points

• Produces highly accurate relevance scores.
• Used after document retrieval.
• Slower than embedding models.
• Improves search quality.

Applications

• Semantic Search
• RAG Systems
• Question Answering
• Search Engines

Example

In a RAG system, FAISS retrieves the top 10 documents. The Cross Encoder reranks them and selects the most relevant ones before sending them to the language model.

Summary

Cross Encoders improve retrieval accuracy by carefully evaluating the relationship between a query and retrieved documents, making them an important component of modern RAG systems.
