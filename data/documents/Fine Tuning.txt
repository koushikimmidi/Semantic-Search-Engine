Fine-Tuning

Definition

Fine-tuning is the process of training a pre-trained AI model on a specific dataset to improve its performance for a particular task.

How it Works

A pre-trained model is loaded and trained further using task-specific data while preserving its existing knowledge.

Key Points

• Uses pre-trained models
• Requires less data than training from scratch
• Improves task-specific performance

Applications

• Chatbots
• Medical AI
• Legal AI
• Code Generation

Example

A language model is fine-tuned on medical documents to answer healthcare-related questions.

Summary

Fine-tuning adapts a general AI model to perform better in a specific domain.
